Bytu Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · version 2026-11-19

This policy explains what personal data the Bytu app processes, why, with whom and for how long, and how you can exercise your rights. It applies to the app on iPhone, iPad and Android and follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018), Brazil's Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents (ECA Digital, Law 15,211/2025), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR and the applicable United States privacy laws. If the Portuguese and English versions differ, the Portuguese version prevails, except where the law of your country requires otherwise.

Summary
  • We do not sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps or websites.
  • The app does not record your voice. Your device's speech recognition turns speech into text, and only the text reaches our servers. The text of what you said is deleted after 30 days.
  • Location is optional, approximate and never stored.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings › Delete account.

2. Data we process

2.1 Account and sign-in

You sign in with Apple (iPhone and iPad only) or Google. We receive the account identifier at the provider, the name and email it shares (Apple may share a relay address that hides yours) and we create a random internal identifier. We never receive your password.

2.2 Profile

Nickname (permanent), date of birth, time zone, language, the form of address you choose (masculine, feminine or neutral), the version and date you accepted these documents, and your country, taken from your store account, your device region or your time zone.

2.3 Age

We use your date of birth and, when the store provides it, the age range shared by Apple (Declared Age Range) or Google (Play Age Signals). When they differ, the stricter reading applies. From this we compute the dates when features unlock. This data is used only to unlock or lock features, never for ads, and is not sent to analytics.

2.4 Voice conversation

When you talk to your Bytu, the system speech recognition service (Apple on iPhone and iPad; Google on Android) turns your speech into text. That service may process the audio on Apple's or Google's servers under their own policies. Bytu does not record or send audio: only the text (up to 1,200 characters) reaches our server, together with the generated reply and the game's decision. The text of what you said is deleted after 30 days. Speech blocked by moderation is not stored.

Your Bytu keeps lessons as predefined items from a catalog, never your words, plus a few structured facts from conversations (for example, a topic of interest). All of it is deleted with your account.

2.5 Game

The state of your Bytu and its family (name, stage, needs, traits, career, achievements, family tree), inventory, coins, daily rewards, minigames and records, events, egg and greenhouse progress, and app preferences (theme, sounds, language).

2.6 Friends

Friend requests, friendships, blocks and reports. An accepted friend sees your nickname, family surname, your Bytu's look and profile summary, and the family tree (with the characters' names, jobs and biographies). Nobody sees your email, date of birth or country. Other players can find you by nickname or by your exact email; email search can be turned off in Settings. The searched text is not stored.

2.7 Purchases

When you buy something, Apple or Google process the payment; we never receive card details. RevenueCat confirms the purchase and tells us the product, platform, transaction identifier and refunds, linked to your internal identifier.

2.8 Notifications

If you allow it, we store the Firebase Cloud Messaging token, a random installation identifier and the platform, to send game notifications. The token is deleted when you sign out. Local notifications (care reminders) stay on the device.

2.9 Approximate location (optional)

Off until you turn it on in Settings. When on, the app reads your approximate position only while it is open (at most once an hour), rounds it to about 11 km and sends it to the server, which rounds it again to about 55 km and requests the weather forecast from MET Norway. The coordinate is never stored or tied to your account; only the region's forecast is cached. Without location, weather follows your device's time zone.

2.10 Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

Usage events (for example, caring, talking, playing a minigame, buying a product), app and device data (version, model, operating system, language), an installation identifier, your internal identifier and the approximate region Google derives from the IP address. Events never include your nickname, email, speech, conversation, date of birth or age range. Ad signals are always off and we do not collect the advertising identifier.

  • European Union, European Economic Area and United Kingdom: off until you check Send usage statistics, at sign-up or in Settings. If your store, device region or time zone points to one of these countries, or if there is no signal at all, the app asks for your consent.
  • Other countries: on by default, based on our legitimate interest in understanding usage and improving the game. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.

2.11 Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the app crashes or hits an error, we send the technical report (error trace, device model and system, app version, installation identifier and your internal identifier). Before sending, we strip emails, tokens and sensitive text.

2.12 Technical logs and support

Our infrastructure keeps short-term technical access logs (such as IP address, date and time) for security and abuse prevention. If you write to us, we process your email and what you send in order to reply.

4. Artificial intelligence

Your Bytu's replies are generated by AI on our server. We send to OpenAI (moderation and reply text) and to TypeSafe (the Jev service, which suggests the character's reaction) the text of your speech and character data (name, stage, mood, traits, career, interests, lessons, language and form of address). We do not send your nickname, email, date of birth or identifier; OpenAI receives only an irreversible derived code used to fight abuse. OpenAI states that it does not use data received through its API to train models. The AI only suggests: the game validates everything before anything changes.

All speech goes through moderation. If it signals a risk of self-harm, the app does not use AI and shows a fixed message with help contacts for your country.

8. Minimum age and teens

Bytu is for people aged 13 or older and is not directed to children. In EU and EEA countries where the age of digital consent is 16 (such as Germany, Ireland, the Netherlands and Poland), sign-up is accepted only from 16. From 13 to 17, the app runs in minor mode, with protections from the start:

  • friends and email search are locked until 16 (18 in Brazil, the EU, the EEA and the UK);
  • location stays off until the user turns it on;
  • at most one server notification a day, never between 9 pm and 8 am;
  • a break reminder after long periods of use;
  • no advertising profile and no random purchases: every product gives fixed items.

Parents and guardians can oversee purchases and screen time with Apple's and Google's parental controls, and may write to us to request access to a teen's data or deletion of the account.

9. Your rights

You may request: confirmation that we process your data; access and a copy; correction; anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data; portability; deletion of data processed on the basis of consent; information about whom we share it with; review of automated decisions; and objection to processing based on legitimate interest. You may also withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what was done before. In the EU and the UK you also have the right to restriction of processing.

In the app: turn off statistics, location, notifications and email search in Settings, or delete the account. For other requests, write to lfapps25@gmail.com from your account email. We may ask you to confirm your identity and we reply within 15 days.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD), to the data protection authority of your EU country or to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK.

10. United States residents

We collect the categories described in section 2: identifiers, account information, in-app activity, purchase data, approximate location (optional) and diagnostics. We do not sell personal data or "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use sensitive data to infer characteristics. Residents of California and other states with privacy laws may request access, correction and deletion through the contact email, without discrimination.

11. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), per-user access control in the database, provider keys kept only on the server, and removal of sensitive data from crash reports. No system is completely secure; if an incident creates a relevant risk, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Device permissions

  • Microphone and speech recognition: to talk by voice.
  • Notifications: for game notices and reminders.
  • Approximate location: optional, for the weather.
  • Photos (add only): to save your Bytu's card if you choose to. The app does not read your library.

Widgets use a local copy of the game state that stays on the device.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Relevant changes will be announced in the app or on this page before they take effect and, where the law requires, we will ask for your consent again. The date at the top shows the version in force.
