Contact us

Questions, problems, purchases that did not arrive, reports and requests about your data: lfapps25@gmail.com. If you can, write from your account email and include your nickname and whether you use iPhone/iPad or Android. We reply within 15 days.

Delete your account and data

In the app: open Settings, tap Delete account and confirm twice. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Without the app: email lfapps25@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account", from the email used to sign in, including your nickname. We confirm the request is yours and delete it within 15 days.

What is deleted: profile, nickname, date of birth, your Bytu and its family, conversations, lessons, friends, blocks, recorded purchases and notification tokens. On iPhone and iPad, Sign in with Apple is also revoked.

What stays for a while: usage statistics (up to 14 months) and crash reports (up to 90 days) already sent; reports about other players (up to 90 days, unlinked from the account); the transaction history at RevenueCat and Apple's and Google's records, which follow their policies.

Deletion does not cancel your subscription: cancel it first in the store (see below).

Purchases and subscription

  • Cancel the subscription: App Store or Google Play, at least 24 hours before renewal.
  • Restore purchases: in the app, under General Store › Restore purchases, with the same store account.
  • Refunds: Apple or Google Play. If that does not work, contact us.

Common issues

  • Voice conversation does not work: allow the microphone and speech recognition in your device settings.
  • I get no notifications: allow Bytu notifications in your device settings.
  • I can't add friends: for minors, friends stay locked until the unlock age.
